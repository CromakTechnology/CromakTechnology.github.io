Frequently Asked Questions

Is DeepClean Suite safe for my Windows Registry?

Yes. Our engine uses an "Atomic Healing" architecture that analyzes only orphaned and redundant keys. Additionally, the system automatically creates a Registry clone (.dcr) before executing any deep alterations.

How do I receive my license after purchasing?

Processing is instantaneous. Once the payment is confirmed, your unique activation key and installation instructions are sent directly to your registered email.

Can I use the license on more than one computer?

It depends on your plan. The Lifetime Personal license allows activation on up to 3 simultaneous computers. Monthly and Annual licenses are restricted to 1 device.

Does the software require an internet connection to work?

Initial activation requires an internet connection, but all cleaning modules, boot optimization, and data wiping operate 100% offline to ensure your absolute privacy.
