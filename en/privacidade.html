Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

1. Zero-Knowledge Philosophy & Local Processing

Cromak Technology develops software with an extreme focus on performance and security. As an engineering principle, most of our tools, including DeepClean Suite and Password Guard, operate on an Offline-First (local processing) basis.

For Password Guard, we utilize a Zero-Knowledge architecture. Your Master Password and vault data are encrypted locally via AES-256 bits. We do not receive, intercept, or hold the keys to decrypt your data. If you lose your Master Password, Cromak cannot recover it.

2. Personal Data Collection

We collect the bare minimum of information required to operate our licenses and technical support:

  • Purchase Data: Name and Email used at the time of license acquisition to send the activation key.
  • Telemetry Data (Optional): Software crash logs, which are fully anonymized and contain no personal file data or user registry entries.
  • Financial Transactions: We do not store credit card data. Processing is handled entirely by third-party encrypted payment gateways.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate and validate your software license on our servers (Hardware ID).
  • Provide human technical support when requested via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Send critical security updates and notices regarding the end-of-life cycle of legacy versions.

4. Data Sharing

Cromak Technology does not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties under any circumstances. We are software engineers, not data brokers.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / LGPD)

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account and email data from our support database at any time by emailing cromaksupport@gmail.com. Note: Deleting your licensing email may make it impossible to recover lost activation keys.
